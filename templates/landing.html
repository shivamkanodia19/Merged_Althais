The problem

A denied claim
does not fix itself.

Every denial needs someone to find the reason, fix it, write the appeal and send it before the deadline. In a small clinic, that person is also answering the phone. So claims sit, and the money stays unpaid.

Mistakes go out

A code pair the payer will not pay for together. A missing detail in the note. Small things that are easy to miss on a busy day.

Denials pile up

Each one needs its own look. With a small staff, the new ones keep arriving while the old ones wait.

Appeals take time

A good appeal letter has to say the right things in the right way. Most offices write each one from scratch.

Billing words, explained

No jargon
left unexplained.

Denied claim
A bill the insurance company refused to pay, usually with a reason code.
Appeal
A letter asking the payer to take a second look and pay.
SOAP note
The standard visit note: what the patient said, what you found, your assessment and your plan.
ICD-10
The codes for the diagnosis: what is wrong with the patient.
CPT
The codes for the procedure: what you did.
Bundling (NCCI)
A Medicare rulebook of code pairs that cannot be billed together. Breaking it is a common reason for denial.
Questions

Questions you
should ask us.

Will this get my denied claims paid?

We cannot promise that, and nobody honest can. Althais helps you catch common mistakes before filing and saves time writing appeals. Whether a payer pays depends on the payer and the claim.

Does Althais see my denials on its own?

Not yet. You tell Althais which claims were denied and paste in the denial text. It drafts the letter from there.

Do I have to replace my EHR or my biller?

No. Althais works beside what you already use. We do not have direct EHR connections yet, so today you work in Althais alongside your EHR. A biller can use it to clean up claims and draft appeals.

Is patient information safe?

Visit and claim data is protected health information, and we treat it that way. Please talk to us about data handling and a business associate agreement before you use real patient information. And please leave patient details out of the pilot sign-up form.

What does it cost?

See our pricing page for current plans. Pilot terms are agreed with each clinic.

Is this just an AI note-taker?

Many AI scribes stop at the note. Althais goes on to suggest codes, check them, and draft the appeal if a claim is denied.
